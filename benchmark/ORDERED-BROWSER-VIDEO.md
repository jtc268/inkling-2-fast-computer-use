# Every browser task in order

[Watch the 2:19 film](https://jtc268.github.io/inkling-2-fast-computer-use/browser-benchmark-in-order.mp4). One chapter per task, starting with form fill and add to cart, then five MiniWoB++ tasks and 26 WebArena IDs. Each chapter labels the hardware, checkpoint and native outcome. The footage plays at 1×. Times are for the full task, and paired successes show a shared clock when the source recordings support it. Two WebArena chapters have only an original run.

The opening 5.18× is the fastest passing pair on MiniWoB++ (19.86 → 3.84 s). The filmed MacBook Pro repeat measured 15.46 → 3.36 s, saving 12.11 seconds.

The revised search chapters use the first paired Mac take, including failures. Green tea first failed as an unseen query, then became a development example. Its chapter keeps that history on screen. Switch accessories and green tea include a second take on RTX 5090. The forum chapter also shows the earlier one-row checkpoint that completed the post.
